Privacy Policy for Ben Harrison Residence

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

  • Personal identification information (e.g., name, contact details, address).

5. Data Protection and Security

  • You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data.
  • You may opt out of certain communications by notifying management.
  • Requests regarding personal data can be made through our contact details below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We reserve the right to update this policy as needed.
  • Residents will be notified of significant changes.
